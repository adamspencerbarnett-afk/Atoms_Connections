# Atom's Connections

**A private social space for your family and friends that runs in your own web browser.**

> **Beta:** Atom's Connections is still in development. It currently runs on Windows laptops and PCs (Chrome or Edge), not on phones or tablets yet. Please send bug reports, wish lists and ideas to **atomsconnections@gmail.com**.

Share photos, videos, links, and thoughts in a feed. Chat one-to-one or in small groups. Like, comment, and repost. It looks and feels like the social apps you know, with one big difference: **there is no company in the middle.** Everything you post stays on your own computer, encrypted, and travels directly to the people you've connected with. Nobody else can see it, collect it, or sell it.

- No account, no password, no sign-up. You type your name and you're in.
- You connect with someone by sending them a one-time invite code.
- Only the people you have personally connected with can see your posts.
- Works on Windows with Chrome or Edge (Firefox works too).

## Download and use

1. **Download** [`Atoms-Connections-Setup.exe`](https://atomsconnections.com/Atoms-Connections-Setup.exe) and run it. It installs just for you (no administrator password), adds Atom's Connections to the Start menu, and can put a shortcut on your desktop.

   Atom's Connections is new, so Windows is careful with it at first: your browser may ask whether to keep the download (choose **Keep**), and Windows may show a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* box. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

   Prefer no installer? Download [`Atoms-Connections.zip`](https://atomsconnections.com/Atoms-Connections.zip), unzip it anywhere and double-click `AtomsConnections`.

2. **Type your name**, pick a photo if you like, and tap **Create my space**.

### Connecting with someone

- Go to **Family/Friends → Invite**. You get a code for one person. Copy it and send it to them by text or email. Tap **Invite** again for the next person; each person needs their own code.
- Received a code? Go to **Family/Friends → Have an invite code?**, paste it, and tap **Connect**. You're connected automatically as soon as you're both online.

### Good to know

- Always open the app from its desktop shortcut or the Start menu, not from a browser bookmark.
- Posts and messages travel while you both have the app open. If someone is away, things wait and arrive the next time you're both online.
- **Settings → Back up now** saves your photos, posts, and chats into your Pictures and Documents folders, plus a password-locked restore file for moving to a new computer. Do this now and then.
- The **Online / Offline** switch at the top disconnects you completely.

### Sharing it with family and friends

Send them the same zip file, or this page. Everyone needs the same version to connect.

## Privacy, in one paragraph

Your identity is a cryptographic key made on your device. Everything stored on your computer is encrypted and locked to your Windows account. Every message between you and a friend is end-to-end encrypted, and media is checked for tampering on arrival. A small public "introduction" service helps two browsers find each other on the internet; it only ever sees two anonymous addresses, never names or content.
