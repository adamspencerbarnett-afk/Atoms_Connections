What it does

Everything you'd expect from a social app. Only the people you'd expect to see it.

It looks and feels like the apps you already know, kept simple on purpose so the whole family can use it, from kids to grandparents.

Photos and videos

Post them to your feed or send them in a chat. Drag them in, paste them, or pick them from a folder.

Links and online videos

Share a YouTube video or a link to anything on the web. It shows up as a neat card your family can play right there.

Chat, one-to-one or as a group

Private conversations with the people you love, plus small group chats for the whole family.

Your people, and only your people

You connect with someone by sending them a one-time invite code. No strangers, no public profiles, no searching.

Likes, comments and reposts

React to each other's posts the familiar way. See who liked and who has seen what you shared.

Catches up while you're away

Posts and messages wait patiently and arrive the next time you and your family are both online.

Safe for everyone

Built private from the ground up, with multiple layers of encryption.

Most social networks keep your photos and messages on their computers and make money from them. Atom's Connections has no company in the middle at all. There's simply nowhere for your family's life to leak out.

1
Stored on your own computer, encrypted

Your posts, photos and chats never leave your device unless you share them, and they're locked to your computer.

2
Sent directly, encrypted end to end

What you share travels straight from your browser to your family's browsers. No one in between can read it, not even us.

3
No accounts, no ads, no tracking

There's nothing to sign up for, nothing to sell, and no one watching. Just the people you invited.

How it works

Up and running in a couple of minutes.

Download and start

Download the zip, unzip it anywhere, and double-click Start Atom's Connections. Type your name and you're in. No account, no password.

Invite your people

Tap Invite to get a private code for one person. Text or email it to them. When they enter it, you're connected automatically.

Share and chat

Post photos, videos and links to your feed, chat directly, and enjoy a social space that belongs to your family alone.

Keep a backup

Your profile and connections live only on your computer. If its browser data is cleared, Windows is reinstalled, or you move to a new PC without a backup, the connection is lost for good and you'll start over with new invites. Settings → Back up now protects you.

Beta: Atom's Connections is still in testing and works on Windows laptops and PCs (Chrome or Edge), not on phones or tablets yet. The first time you start it, Windows may show a "protected your PC" notice, as it does for any new program from the internet: click More info, then Run anyway. The download includes a short guide and the user agreement.
Games and tools

Play together, right inside your family's space.

Plugins are small games and tools that live in the app. Add one with a click, open it, or tap Play with… to invite family who are online; they join from a popup and you're playing together. Every plugin runs in a sealed box: it can't see your posts, chats or the internet, so the fun stays as private as everything else.

Family fun, built in

PAC Runner comes with the app: a neon maze for one to four players, where everyone races to grab the coins while the ghosts give chase. Open a treasure chest and the ghosts turn blue and run from you. Every maze you clear earns an extra life, a brand-new maze and a little more speed. Grandparents and grandkids can run the same maze from their own homes, scores side by side. It's the kind of five-minute game that turns "how was school?" into an evening together.

How plugins work

  • Add a plugin file someone gave you, or make your own: it's an ordinary web page packed into one file.
  • Open it to play alone, or choose Play with… and pick family who are online. Joining is one tap.
  • Share a plugin to your feed or a chat; it travels directly to the people you choose, like a photo.
  • Not just games: the same sealed box can hold family tools, like a shared shopping list or a chores board.

PAC Runner is also the worked example for anyone who wants to build a game. The app includes a short developer guide and the game's full source.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?
Yes. Use it for free and share unchanged copies with your family and friends at no cost. It just can't be modified or sold. The full agreement comes with the download.
Who can see what I post?
Only the people you've connected with. And they only see each other's likes and comments if they're connected with each other too. Direct relationships are all that count.
Where is my data kept?
On your own computer, encrypted. There's a one-click backup to your Pictures and Documents folders, and a password-locked restore file for moving to a new PC.
Does everyone need to be online at once?
No. If someone is away, your posts and messages wait and arrive the next time you're both online, like leaving a note on the fridge.
What about Mac users?
The one-click start is for Windows. On a Mac, open the included index.html in Safari or Chrome; everything works the same way.
Can I use it on my phone or tablet?
Not yet. During beta testing it runs on Windows laptops and PCs. A phone-friendly version is planned.
Can kids use it?
That's the idea. There's no public space, no strangers and no algorithm; a child only ever sees the family and friends you connected them with. And because every connection begins with an invite code that someone has to hand over, parents hold the keys. When your child wants to add a school friend, the invite passes from parent to parent rather than from child to child: the two families meet first, connect with each other, and only then do the kids. Every friendship in the app has a grown-up on both ends who knows about it.
